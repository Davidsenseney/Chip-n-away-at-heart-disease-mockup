Join the Heart Health Initiative

Volunteering with Chip ‘N Away at Heart Disease is a meaningful way to support efforts focused on the early detection, risk reduction, and prevention of cardiovascular disease in your community. Volunteers contribute through event support, community outreach, and health education, helping raise awareness of key risk factors such as hypertension, high cholesterol, and lifestyle-related contributors. By sharing reliable, evidence-based information, you can help individuals make informed health decisions, adopt healthier habits, and recognize the importance of early intervention.

If you’re interested in making a lasting impact on community heart health, we invite you to complete the volunteer sign-up form below. Stay connected with Chip ‘N Away at Heart Disease on Facebook and Instagram for updates, events, and community health resources.

Benefits of Volunteering

Volunteering with our organization offers a meaningful way to make a direct impact in the fight against cardiovascular diseases. We organize a variety of outreach programs and campaigns with medical professionals and our associates aimed at raising awareness about the impact of cardiovascular diseases. These initiatives allow us to make referrals and provide actionable, education-based strategies that help individuals prevent heart-related health emergencies.

Beyond community impact, volunteering also helps you build valuable skills while gaining experience working alongside healthcare professionals. It is also an opportunity to grow your network and become more engaged in public health advocacy. Most importantly, your involvement contributes to lasting change—helping save lives through awareness and prevention.

Impact & Stories

Cynthia Malone serves as the Executive Director of Chip’n Away @ Heart Disease, a non-profit organization she co-founded with her late husband, Coach Narleski “Chip” Malone. Through her unwavering dedication to heart health advocacy, Cynthia has reached over 78,000 individuals via community outreach and educational events.

In 2023-2024 alone, Chip’n Away @ Heart Disease has impacted the lives of 7,460 people, spreading a vital message of hope for heart health. Cynthia’s engaging speaking style blends humor and encouragement, resonating with audiences and earning her numerous callbacks and positive feedback. She is grateful for the strength of the collaborative alliance that works with her to achieve the organization’s goals. She emphasizes the importance of community support in combating heart disease.

Over 78,000 lives touched through actionable education and community support.
