IN LOVING MEMORY

Coach Narleski "Chip" Malone

Founder of Chip'n Away @ Heart Disease

Coach Narleski “Chip” Malone was a basketball coach who impacted many people’s lives.

In 2010, Chip was diagnosed with heart disease and underwent a heart transplant. This life-changing event deeply impacted both Chip and his wife Cynthia, inspiring them to fight back against heart disease.

Together, they started "Chip'n Away @ Heart Disease" to raise awareness, support patients and families, and honor the fight against this silent killer.

Even though Coach Chip has passed, his memory lives on through this organization and all the lives he continues to touch.
